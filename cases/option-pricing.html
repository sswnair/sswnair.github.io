Apple Option Pricing and Greeks

I priced a selected Apple call and put with Black-Scholes, checked put-call parity and traced how delta, gamma, theta, vega and rho change with the inputs.

Pricing result

At a $336.91 spot price, $335 strike and 27.68% volatility, Black-Scholes produces a $26.47 call and $19.82 put. The ten-step binomial call is $26.12, and put-call parity reconciles within rounding.

The downside, base and upside call values are $10.37, $26.47 and $60.96. No dated option-chain midquote is embedded, so these are model outputs under the stated inputs rather than a claim about a live quoted option.

Limits of the pricing framework

  • The ten-step tree is a coarse cross-check rather than a production lattice.
