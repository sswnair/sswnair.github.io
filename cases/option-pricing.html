Model view · The model prices the options consistently and passes parity checks

Conclusion

At a $336.91 spot price, $335 strike and 27.68% volatility, the model values the call at $26.47 and the put at $19.82. Put-call parity reconciles within rounding.

Call delta is 0.58. The pricing volatility is also the closest solver-table value for the reference call premium over the selected term to December 18, 2026.

Risks and limitations

  • The option inputs are a dated market snapshot.
  • Black-Scholes assumes continuous trading and lognormal returns.
  • The model does not capture volatility skew or discrete jump risk.
  • The output is a valuation exercise, not a trading recommendation.
