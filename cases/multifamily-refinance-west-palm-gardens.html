West Palm Gardens Debt Refinance and Lender Reporting

A lender-facing model for 437 units covering NOI, DSCR, debt yield, cash monitoring and refinance capacity.

Self-directed public-source case studyPrepared through June 2026

Underwriting question

Does the $50.0m loan remain within the model's leverage, coverage and debt-yield screens?

$50.0m Loan balance Interest-only loan
1.66x NOI DSCR FY2025
10.9% NOI debt yield FY2025
59.4% Appraisal-based LTV 2024 appraisal
Underwriting view · The loan remains within the modelled credit screens

Conclusion

FY2025 NOI supports 1.66x NOI DSCR, 1.62x NCF DSCR and 10.9% NOI debt yield.

The 65% LTV screen is binding and implies $54.73m of capacity, or $4.73m of headroom, using the 2024 appraisal. This is a planning screen, not a lender commitment.

Risks and limitations

  • LTV and loan capacity use a 2024 appraisal, not a June 2026 valuation.
  • Lender thresholds are planning screens, not contractual terms.
  • The model does not establish current refinancing pricing or fees.
  • No binding loan commitment is implied.
