Verizon Credit Analysis

A public-data credit case covering cash flow, leverage, coverage, covenants, debt capacity and downside.

Credit question

Does recurring cash generation provide enough capacity to reduce leverage and preserve coverage?

2.73x 2026E net leverage Down from 2.91x
7.63x 2026E coverage EBITDA / cash interest
2.97x 2026E DSCR Base case
2.22x 2030E net leverage Model path
Credit view · Base-case credit remains defensible

Conclusion

Net leverage falls from 2.91x in 2025 to 2.73x in 2026E and 2.22x by 2030E. The model produces 7.63x interest coverage and 2.97x DSCR for 2026E.

Recurring wireless cash flow supports the case. The main constraints are high absolute debt, capital intensity and shareholder distributions competing with debt reduction.

Risks and limitations

  • Forecasts and covenant thresholds are analyst assumptions.
  • The model does not reproduce every legal definition in the debt documents.
  • Capital intensity can reduce discretionary cash flow.
  • Verizon reported Q2 2026 results on 24 July 2026, after this FY2025-based model was frozen. The forecast is retained as a historical credit exercise and is not an agency rating.
