Corporate credit · Verizon Communications

Verizon Credit Analysis

I assessed Verizon's cash flow, leverage, interest coverage and debt capacity against clearly labeled internal credit thresholds.

Self-directed public-data case studyFY2025 reported base with analytical forecasts through 2030E

Credit question

Does recurring cash generation provide enough capacity to reduce leverage and preserve coverage?

2.73x 2026E net leverage 3.75x internal maximum
7.63x 2026E interest coverage 4.00x internal minimum
2.97x 2026E DSCR 1.30x internal minimum
3.10x Downside net leverage 2026 downside
The base and downside cases stay inside all three internal credit thresholds

Credit view

The 2026E base case carries 2.73x net leverage against a 3.75x maximum, 7.63x interest coverage against a 4.00x minimum and 2.97x DSCR against a 1.30x minimum. Net leverage declines to 2.22x by 2030E.

The 2026 downside reaches 3.10x net leverage and 5.73x interest coverage, still within the analytical limits. High absolute debt, capital intensity and shareholder distributions remain the principal constraints.

What could weaken the credit view

  • The thresholds are analytical screens, not contractual covenants or agency criteria.
  • Capital intensity, spectrum spending and shareholder distributions can slow deleveraging.
  • Reported and adjusted debt definitions require reconciliation before comparison with company or agency leverage measures.
