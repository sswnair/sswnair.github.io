West Palm Gardens Value-Add Renovation and Capital Plan

A phased renovation model covering unit counts, capital spending, NOI uplift, debt sizing and projected returns.

Self-directed public-source case studyPrepared through June 2026; renovation count dated August 2024

Decision question

Does the remaining renovation plan generate enough NOI uplift to justify the scheduled capital?

Model view · Positive modeled yield, subject to a current scope refresh

Conclusion

The illustrative plan schedules $5.19m of remaining capital and $542,000 of annual NOI uplift, producing a 10.4% untrended yield on cost.

The 252-unit remaining balance is dated August 2024. Renovation progress, achievable rents and construction costs must be refreshed before treating the return as decision-ready.

Risks and limitations

  • The remaining-unit count is from August 2024.
  • Rent uplift uses observed in-place differences, not a fresh market-rent study.
  • Future cost, pace, occupancy and exit assumptions are planning inputs.
  • The model does not separately include unit downtime, turnover costs, leasing costs or taxes.
