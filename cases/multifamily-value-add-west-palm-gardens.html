West Palm Gardens Value-Add Renovation and Capital Plan

What's left of the renovation program, counted by the door. Cost per unit, rent lift, NOI uplift, what it does to debt capacity, and what the equity gets for the trouble.

Does the remaining renovation plan generate enough NOI uplift to justify the scheduled capital?

Renovation result

The illustrative plan schedules $5.19m of remaining capital and $542,000 of annual NOI uplift, producing a 10.4% untrended yield on cost.

The 252-unit remaining balance is dated August 2024. Renovation progress, achievable rents and construction costs must be refreshed before treating the return as decision-ready.
