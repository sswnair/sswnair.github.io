Investment view · The growth case needs substantial additional financing

Conclusion

The model reaches $1.70bn of ARR by 2031. It requires $38.6m of additional financing in 2026, followed by assumed later rounds to keep cash positive.

Founder ownership declines to 21.7% at exit. Investor returns are exceptionally high under the assumed entry prices and exit value, so this case is presented as funding and dilution mechanics, not as an investment recommendation.

What could change the answer

Risks and limitations

  • The company, funding schedule, valuation inputs and cash-gap round are illustrative analyst assumptions.
  • Preferred-share rights are simplified; no company-specific term sheet is represented.
  • The cash runway remains positive only because the modelled rounds occur as scheduled.
  • Exit-based investor returns are highly sensitive to timing and valuation.
