SaaS Funding and Cap Table Mechanics

Seed to Series C, priced round by round. The cash need keeps growing and the founders keep shrinking.

Illustrative case using public SaaS benchmarksModel date August 25, 2026; forecast through 2031E

Investment question

How do the planned rounds and seed top-up affect runway and founder ownership?

The plan needs a $38.61m top-up and leaves founders at 24.03%

Funding and ownership result

The operating plan needs a $38.61m seed top-up in 2026 to keep cash above the modeled minimum. Across Seed, Series A, B and C plus the top-up, total new money is $343.61m.

The top-up is priced from an $80m pre-money valuation and gives the additional investor 32.55%. Founders move from 85.0% initially to 24.03% at exit. The growth case exists to drive the financing mechanics and is not presented as an investment forecast.

Venture views

Funding rounds, runway and dilution

Cash needs set the round size, sequential pricing sets new-investor ownership and the cap table carries that dilution through exit.

Cap-table limits

What the cap table leaves out

  • Operating, financing and exit assumptions are illustrative.
  • The cap table omits liquidation preferences, convertibles, option-pool refreshes and follow-on rights.
  • Scenario ownership stays fixed, so operating outcomes do not trigger additional funding rounds.
