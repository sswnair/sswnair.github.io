Plan result

The FY2027 base plan produces $734.67bn of revenue, $29.39bn of operating income and $10.90bn of planning free cash flow. Ending cash reaches $21.63bn.

The downside still generates $4.25bn of planning free cash flow and ends with $14.98bn of cash, while the upside reaches $16.07bn and $26.79bn. The first-half comparison is an illustrative operating scenario, not reported monthly actuals.
