Conclusion

The illustrative fund produces a 1.96x gross MOIC and 13.3% gross IRR. After $93.9m of management fees and $0.0m of modelled carried interest under the supplied waterfall, net IRR is 9.6%.

The repaired scenario table produces 5.4% net IRR in the downside, 9.6% in the detailed base case and 21.5% in the upside. Exit multiple and timing remain the central variables.

What could change the answer

Risks and limitations

  • The fund and portfolio companies are illustrative, and the downside and upside cases use blended liquidation assumptions rather than forecasts.
  • The whole-fund European waterfall is simplified and does not model recycling, clawback, escrow, tax allocations or fund-specific LPA provisions.
  • Carried interest is $0.0m under the supplied assumptions because the simplified waterfall does not clear its preferred-return threshold; this is a model result, not a missing formula.
  • Exit proceeds remain assumption-driven, and past fund structures do not guarantee future outcomes.
