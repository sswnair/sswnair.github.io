Fund return

The detailed schedule produces a 13.3% gross IRR and an 8.3% net IRR after $93.9m of management fees and $77.9m of carried interest. LP distributions are $953.1m and TVPI is 1.49x.

The waterfall now uses a period-end cash-flow convention, so a capital call does not earn a full year of preferred return on the date it is funded. Distribution, GP-entitlement and ending-preference checks reconcile.
