How do segment growth, margins, working capital and capital expenditure flow through the three statements?

Finance view · Cash generation rises, with capital expenditure as the key watch item

Conclusion

The base case grows revenue from $281.7bn in FY2025A to $428.4bn in FY2030E. EBITDA margin reaches 57.8%, while free cash flow reaches $158.0bn by FY2030E.

The main forecast question is whether cloud growth can support continued infrastructure spending. A review should focus on Intelligent Cloud growth, capital expenditure intensity and cash conversion.

What could change the answer

Risks and limitations

  • Long-range demand for cloud services and data-centre capacity is uncertain.
  • Capital expenditure may remain elevated for longer than modelled.
  • Other long-term liabilities remain a residual forecast category in this simplified model.
  • Dividends and repurchases are not modelled, so ending cash accumulates under the supplied forecast structure.
  • Microsoft reported FY2026 actual results on 29 July 2026, after this FY2025-based model was frozen. The forecast is retained as a historical modelling exercise and is not presented as current consensus or company guidance.
