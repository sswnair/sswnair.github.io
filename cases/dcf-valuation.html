Apple DCF Valuation

I valued Apple from forecast unlevered cash flow using Gordon-growth and exit-multiple terminal values, then reconciled enterprise value to equity value.

Self-directed public-data case studyFY2025 financial base; July 27, 2026 share price

Valuation question

What share value is supported by the model's cash-flow forecast and terminal assumptions?

$127.64 Implied share price Selected terminal methods
$336.91 Market price July 27, 2026 close
62.1% Discount to market Base DCF
8.91% WACC Base case
The DCF supports $127.64 per share, well below the July market price

Valuation result

At an 8.91% WACC, the selected terminal methods produce $1.88tn of enterprise value, $1.92tn of equity value and $127.64 per share. That is 62.1% below the $336.91 price used in the case.

The Gordon-growth cases range from $90.14 to $171.82 per share. Every displayed case remains below the market input, and the result moves sharply with WACC, terminal growth and long-run margin assumptions.

What could change the valuation

  • The market price and risk inputs remain fixed at their stated July 2026 dates.
