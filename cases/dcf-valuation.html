Valuation question

What share value is supported by the model's cash-flow forecast and terminal assumptions?

$127.64 Implied share price Blended terminal methods
$336.91 Model market price 27 July 2026 close
8.9% WACC Base case
2.5% Terminal growth Base case
Valuation view · The base DCF does not support the model market price

Conclusion

At an 8.9% WACC and 2.5% terminal growth rate, the repaired blended DCF produces $127.64 per share. The $336.91 market price used in the model leaves a 62.1% gap.

The sensitivity tables show what must change to close that gap. The result is most exposed to WACC, terminal growth, exit multiple and long-run operating margins.

What could change the answer

Risks and limitations

  • Small changes in terminal assumptions move value materially.
  • The risk-free rate, beta and pre-tax cost-of-debt proxy lack exact observation dates; the debt proxy also lacks a quoted bond basket.
  • The Damodaran equity-risk-premium vintage was not recorded, so the 8.9% WACC cannot be reproduced to a complete market-data snapshot.
  • This is a dated forecast snapshot, not a current recommendation. It should be read with trading comparables and business-quality analysis.
