Apple DCF Valuation

An unlevered DCF with a five-year forecast, WACC, two terminal methods, an equity bridge and sensitivities.

Self-directed public-data case studyMarket-price input: June 26, 2026

Decision question

What share value is supported by the model's cash-flow forecast and terminal assumptions?

$155.55 Implied share price Blended terminal methods
$283.78 Model market price June 26, 2026 input
8.9% WACC Base case
2.5% Terminal growth Base case
Model view · The base DCF does not support the model market price

Conclusion

At an 8.9% WACC and 2.5% terminal growth rate, the blended DCF produces $155.55 per share. The market price used in the model is $283.78, leaving a 45.2% gap.

The sensitivity tables show what must change to close that gap. The result is most exposed to WACC, terminal growth, exit multiple and long-run operating margins.

Risks and limitations

  • Small changes in terminal assumptions move value materially.
  • Forecasts are analyst estimates.
  • The model does not assign a separate value to future strategic options.
  • The DCF should be read with trading comps and business-quality analysis.
