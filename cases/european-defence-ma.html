M&A screening · Thales, Rheinmetall and RENK

European Defense M&A Target Screen

The group screened Rheinmetall and RENK as potential targets using DCF, trading comps, precedents, synergies, financing, leverage and accretion.

Academic transaction case studyValuation date July 20, 2026 using FY2025 reporting and FY2026 guidance

Transaction question

Do the modeled Rheinmetall or RENK acquisition cases create value for Thales?

€1,118.52 Rheinmetall DCF Blended value per share
8.00x Rheinmetall leverage Pro forma net debt/EBITDA
€58.44 RENK DCF Blended value per share
1.56x RENK leverage Pro forma net debt/EBITDA
Neither target clears the modeled screen at the stated terms

Target-screen result

Rheinmetall's proposed terms imply an 88.3% premium to blended DCF, 81.9% first-year dilution and 8.00x pro forma net debt to EBITDA. It fails the price, earnings and leverage screens by a wide margin.

RENK is less leveraged at 1.56x, but still carries a 34.6% premium to blended DCF and 7.7% first-year dilution. At these case terms, the better response is to reprice or restructure rather than proceed.

Screening limits

What the screen does not establish

  • Unaffected share prices and acquisition premiums are editable case assumptions, not current quotes.
  • Synergies and financing terms are academic inputs rather than announced transaction terms.
  • Execution, regulation and defense-sector policy require separate diligence.
