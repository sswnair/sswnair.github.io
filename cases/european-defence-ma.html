M&A screening · Thales, Rheinmetall and RENK

European Defence M&A Screening

A two-target screen using DCF, public comps, precedents, synergies, financing, leverage and accretion.

Illustrative academic transaction caseAnalysis frozen as of 20 July 2026

Transaction question

Do the modelled Rheinmetall or RENK acquisition cases create value for Thales?

88.3% Rheinmetall premium To blended DCF
8.0x Rheinmetall leverage Pro forma net debt / EBITDA
7.7% RENK dilution Year one
1.6x RENK leverage Pro forma net debt / EBITDA
Deal view · Reject Rheinmetall terms and reprice RENK

Conclusion

The Rheinmetall case requires an 88.3% premium to blended DCF, produces 81.9% year-one dilution and raises pro forma net debt / EBITDA to 8.0x. The model says not to pursue the case terms.

The RENK case is less leveraged at 1.6x net debt / EBITDA, but still produces 7.7% year-one dilution and ROIC below WACC. The terms need to be repriced or restructured.
