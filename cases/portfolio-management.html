Portfolio result

The embedded six-asset proxy series produces a modeled 12.82% annualized return, 7.32% volatility, 1.22 Sharpe ratio and 4.40% maximum drawdown.

Every exposure and benchmark is an illustrative proxy. The workbook demonstrates portfolio mechanics and must be rerun with dated market data before any historical-performance claim.
