Academy Sports LBO

A public-data LBO with sources and uses, two debt tranches, cash sweep, checks and sponsor returns.

Decision question

Does a take-private at 8.5x entry EBITDA produce an acceptable sponsor return?

12.4% Sponsor IRR Modeled hold
1.79x Sponsor MOIC Base case
5.5x Initial leverage Debt / EBITDA
8.0x Exit multiple Base case
Model view · Do not pursue at the modeled entry price

Conclusion

With 5.5x initial debt / EBITDA and an 8.0x exit multiple, the base case produces a 12.4% sponsor IRR and 1.79x MOIC. The forecast is a historical case built from 2025E inputs.

Debt paydown is not enough to reach a normal buyout return. The case needs a lower entry price, stronger EBITDA growth or a specific operating plan.
