Academy Sports LBO

I underwrote Academy Sports as a public-data take-private, linking entry valuation, two debt tranches, cash sweep, exit value and sponsor returns.

Self-directed public-data case studyFY2024 sources with 2025E to 2029E historical case forecasts

Investment question

Does a take-private at 8.5x entry EBITDA produce an acceptable sponsor return?

8.5x Entry multiple Entry EBITDA
12.4% Sponsor IRR Core case
1.79x Sponsor MOIC Core case
(2.7%) Downside IRR Downside case
A 12.4% IRR does not justify the modeled entry price

Sponsor return

An 8.5x entry multiple and 8.0x exit multiple produce a 1.79x MOIC and 12.4% sponsor IRR. Sources and uses show $5.66bn of entry enterprise value and $2.42bn of sponsor equity.

The downside case falls to a negative 2.7% IRR. The upside reaches 25.2%, but only with faster growth, margin expansion and a higher exit multiple. Entry price and an executable operating plan have to do more of the work.
