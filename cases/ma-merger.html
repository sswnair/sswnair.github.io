M&A · Cisco acquisition of Splunk

Cisco and Splunk Merger Analysis

Cisco bought Splunk in March 2024. This rebuilds the deal after the fact, from purchase price and financing through goodwill and synergies, to see where the earnings actually land.

Retrospective self-directed case studyTransaction completed March 18, 2024; FY2026E to FY2028E historical case forecasts

Transaction question

How do purchase accounting, financing and synergy delivery affect Cisco's pro forma EPS?

$27.09bn Purchase consideration Final modeled consideration
172.55m Transaction shares Implied at $157 per share
7.2% FY2026 dilution Core case
$19.30bn Goodwill Purchase accounting
Every displayed FY2026 case remains dilutive

Accretion result

The $27.09bn consideration reconciles exactly to the $157 offer price through 172.55m implied transaction shares. Splunk's standalone diluted share count is shown separately from the deal share count.

FY2026 dilution is 7.2% in the core case, 9.7% in the downside and 5.4% in the upside. Even the strongest displayed financing and synergy case does not turn accretive.
