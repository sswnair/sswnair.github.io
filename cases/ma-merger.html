Cisco and Splunk Merger Analysis

A merger model covering purchase price, financing, goodwill, synergies and pro forma earnings.

Retrospective self-directed case studyAnalysis frozen at FY2025 source data; FY2026 to FY2028 remain forecast

Transaction question

How do purchase accounting, financing and synergy delivery affect Cisco's pro forma EPS?

0.9% FY2028 dilution Gap narrows
$12.97bn New debt Modelled financing
Deal view · Modelled dilution narrows, but persists through FY2028

Conclusion

The retrospective model produces 7.2% dilution in FY2026E, 3.6% in FY2027E and 0.9% in FY2028E as assumed synergies phase in.

The main deal variables are synergy timing, financing cost and intangible-asset amortization. Under these assumptions, the earnings gap narrows but does not turn accretive within the forecast period.

Risks and limitations

  • Synergy timing and financing mix are analyst assumptions.
  • The Downside and Upside cases are analyst overlays, not observed deal terms.
  • Purchase accounting is simplified, and integration costs and dis-synergies are not fully captured.
  • Cisco reported FY2026 actual results on 12 August 2026, after this FY2025-based retrospective model was frozen. FY2026E is preserved as a historical forecast, not a current estimate.
