Private Market Liquidity in Public View

My MSc Finance dissertation examines whether interest-rate shocks are associated with wider discounts in 25 London-listed private-equity vehicles.

MSc Finance dissertationSubmitted July 2026

Research question

Are UK interest-rate changes associated with wider listed private equity discounts, and do public prices lead later NAV marks?

25 Vehicles London-listed private equity vehicles
970 Fund-quarters 2015Q2 to 2025Q4

Discounts carry information about later NAV marks

Main empirical result

In a fund fixed-effects panel, a one percentage point rise in the 10-year gilt yield is associated with a price-to-NAV discount 6.1 percentage points wider. The estimate is significant under Driscoll-Kraay inference (p = 0.033) and marginal under fund-and-quarter clustering (p = 0.076), so it is treated as associational rather than causal.

The central result is that current discounts forecast next-quarter NAV returns. The deepest-discount quintile averages −1.0%, versus +4.2% for the narrowest, a 5.2 percentage point endpoint spread. The predicted market-frictions amplification is not supported.

Research design

Interpretation

Limits to interpretation

  • The regressions establish association rather than causation.
  • The quarterly panel is unbalanced and limited to 25 London-listed vehicles.
  • NAV timing creates mechanical staleness that cannot be interpreted as managerial delay.
  • Licensed Morningstar and Refinitiv extracts remain private.
