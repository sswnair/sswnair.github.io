Selected trading reference

The selected 16.22x median EV/EBITDA multiple implies $360.24 per Microsoft share. EPS reconciles to net income and diluted shares, and every market observation and financial row has a recorded date or period.

All ten closing prices are synchronized to June 26, 2026. The operating inputs still span FY2024 and FY2025, so this remains a valuation-method case rather than a live trading recommendation.

Where the comparison can mislead

  • The target and peers share a market date but not a common financial period.
  • Several EBITDA inputs are reference estimates rather than directly reported GAAP measures.
  • The peer set spans software, cloud platforms and large-cap technology with different margins and growth rates.
