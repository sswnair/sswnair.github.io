Microsoft Comparable Companies

Peer screening, market data, trading multiples, outlier review and valuation across five methods.

Decision question

What valuation range is supported by selected software, cloud and large-cap technology peers?

16.2x Peer EV / EBITDA Median
$360.24 Implied share price EV / EBITDA method
6.8x Peer EV / revenue Median
9 Peer set Excluding target
Model view · Use EV / EBITDA as the primary anchor

Conclusion

The peer median is 16.2x EV / EBITDA, which implies $360.24 per share. The other methods range from $263.79 on EV / Revenue to $470.61 on EV / EBIT.

EV / EBITDA is the selected primary reference for this model. Revenue, P / E and P / B remain cross-checks because the companies have different margins, capital structures and accounting profiles.
