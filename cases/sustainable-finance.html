Can the portfolio improve risk-adjusted return and funded-ratio resilience while meeting its climate objectives?

The return-seeking mix raises the carbon-intensity proxy

Allocation result

Current weighted average carbon intensity is 111.25, compared with 129.85 for the return-seeking mix. Coverage is 85% and 96%, so the result is directionally useful but not a complete portfolio footprint.

A 50% reduction from the current proxy sets a 2030 target of 55.62. After correcting the lognormal percentile calculation, the 2030 funded ratio ranges from 0.903x at P10 to 1.131x at P50 and 1.416x at P90.

Limits of the climate and ALM inputs
