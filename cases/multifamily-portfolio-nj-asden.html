Portfolio asset management · Six-property New Jersey portfolio

NJ Asden Multifamily Portfolio Asset Management and Hold/Sell

I linked six New Jersey assets to one portfolio debt structure and compared the present value of holding with current net sale equity.

Self-directed public-source case studyPrepared through April 2026

Investment question

Do modeled hold cash flows and refinance capacity justify retaining the 1,011-unit portfolio?

$7.76m Latest T12 NOI Twelve months to September 2025
1.33x NCF DSCR Latest T12
$89.03m Refinance capacity Modeled for 2029
($7.80m) Hold less sell Portfolio-level screen
The portfolio-level screen favors a sale review

Hold-versus-sell result

Latest T12 NOI is $7.76m and NCF DSCR is 1.33x. Modeled 2029 refinance capacity is $89.03m against a $90.0m loan.

At a 10% discount rate and 6.25% exit cap, present value of hold cash flows is $7.80m below modeled net sale equity. The result supports a portfolio-level sale review, not an asset-level directive.
