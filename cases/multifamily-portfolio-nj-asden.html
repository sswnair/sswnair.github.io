NJ Asden Multifamily Portfolio Asset Management and Hold/Sell

A portfolio model linking property operations, reserves, debt, valuation, refinance capacity and hold/sell economics.

Underwriting question

Do modelled hold cash flows and refinance capacity justify retaining the 1,011-unit portfolio?

$7.76m Latest T12 NOI Twelve months to September 2025
1.33x NCF DSCR Latest T12
$89.03m Refinance capacity Modelled for 2029
($7.80m) Hold less sell Portfolio-level screen
Underwriting view · The portfolio-level screen favours a sale review

Conclusion

Latest T12 NOI is $7.76m and NCF DSCR is 1.33x. Modelled 2029 refinance capacity is $89.03m against a $90.0m loan.

At a 10% discount rate and 6.25% exit cap, present value of hold cash flows is $7.80m below modelled net sale equity. The result supports a portfolio-level sale review, not an asset-level directive.
