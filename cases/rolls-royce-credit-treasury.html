Rolls-Royce Credit and Treasury Review

Rolls-Royce is paying out again. This follows the cash, the debt, the liquidity and the maturity wall to work out how much room is left if the downside turns up.

Cash-floor coverage is 1.43x, below the 1.50x guardrail

Treasury view

Pro forma gross liquidity is £8.56bn and gross sources-to-uses coverage is 2.47x. After preserving the £5.00bn cash floor, coverage falls to 1.43x and misses the 1.50x internal guardrail.

The current 2026 distribution plan equals 101% of FY2025 free cash flow and rises to 194% of free cash flow in the downside. Capping distributions at 50% of prior-year free cash flow would retain about £1.67bn for maturities and operating resilience.
