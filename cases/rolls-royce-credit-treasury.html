Rolls-Royce Credit and Treasury Review

A credit and treasury review covering ratings, cash quality, debt, liquidity, maturities and payout guardrails.

Decision question

Are liquidity and leverage strong enough to support the current refinancing and distribution plans?

£3.27bn FY2025 free cash flow Reported measure
£1.90bn FY2025 net cash Year end
£8.56bn Gross liquidity Pro forma
1.39x Cash-floor coverage Below 1.50x guardrail
Model view · Capital allocation is the main risk

Conclusion

FY2025 free cash flow is £3.27bn and net cash is £1.90bn. Pro forma gross liquidity is £8.56bn, while cash-floor-adjusted sources / uses is 1.39x, below the model's 1.50x guardrail.

The base 2026 case keeps adjusted debt / EBITDA at 0.83x. In the downside, distributions reach 220% of free cash flow. The case recommendation is to cap distributions at 50% of prior-year free cash flow and protect liquidity for the 2027 to 2028 maturities.
