The Louisa Multifamily Acquisition and Hold Underwriting

A 242-unit acquisition and hold model linking operations, interest-only debt, exit value and downside returns.

Self-directed public-source case studyPrepared through Q1 2026

Underwriting question

Does the operating recovery support the 2024 acquisition basis and modelled exit?

$86.28m Total basis 2024 acquisition basis
1.42x Q1 2026 NOI DSCR Annualised quarter
0.82x Equity multiple Modelled exit
(4.0%) Levered XIRR Modelled exit
Underwriting view · Operating recovery does not restore the modelled equity basis

Conclusion

Occupancy improved from 93.0% at acquisition to 95.4% in Q1 2026, and annualised Q1 NOI supports 1.42x DSCR.

The base exit produces a 0.82x equity multiple and negative 4.0% XIRR. The model is therefore a downside hold and recovery case, not a successful acquisition recommendation.

Risks and limitations

  • Q1 2026 revenue and expenses are annualised from one quarter.
  • Residential and retail income are split using the acquisition rent mix.
  • Growth, exit cap rate, selling costs and capital spending are planning assumptions.
  • The model combines the 2024 acquisition basis with later operating results.
