Multifamily acquisition · The Louisa, Portland, Oregon

The Louisa Multifamily Acquisition and Hold Underwriting

Bought in 2024, and by Q1 2026 the operations had recovered. The equity had not. This one tests whether the second thing follows from the first.

Self-directed public-source case studyPrepared through Q1 2026

Investment question

Does the operating recovery support the 2024 acquisition basis and modeled exit?

$86.28m Total basis 2024 acquisition basis
1.42x Q1 2026 NOI DSCR Annualized quarter
0.82x Equity multiple Modeled exit
(4.0%) Levered XIRR Modeled exit
Operating recovery does not restore the modeled equity basis

Hold-case result

Occupancy improved from 93.0% at acquisition to 95.4% in Q1 2026, and annualized Q1 NOI supports 1.42x DSCR.

The base exit produces a 0.82x equity multiple and negative 4.0% XIRR. The model is therefore a downside hold and recovery case, not a successful acquisition recommendation.

What could change the hold result

  • Q1 2026 revenue and expenses are annualized from one quarter.
  • Residential and retail income are split using the acquisition rent mix.
  • The case combines a 2024 basis with later operating data and therefore measures recovery rather than a new acquisition.
