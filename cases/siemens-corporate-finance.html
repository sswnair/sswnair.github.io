Siemens Corporate Finance and Capital Allocation

A scenario-led review of free cash flow, distributions, retained cash and strategic investment capacity.

Academic group projectAnalysis frozen as of 20 July 2026; Q3 results released 6 August are excluded

Management question

Can Siemens fund strategic investment and shareholder returns while protecting cash generation?

€84.4bn FY2026E revenue Base case
€10.8bn FY2026E free cash flow Base case
7.0% Revenue growth Base-case assumption
6% to 8% Guidance range Company outlook used
Finance view · Base-case cash flow supports investment with payout discipline

Conclusion

The base case estimates FY2026E revenue of €84.4bn and free cash flow of €10.8bn. Distribution and strategic investment capacity are scenario outputs, not company guidance.

The severe downside reduces free cash flow to €6.0bn. Payout and acquisition decisions should therefore be tied to scenario cash flow, not the prior year's record result.
