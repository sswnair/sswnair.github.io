Financial statement analysis · NEXT plc and JD Sports Fashion plc

NEXT and JD Sports Financial Statement Analysis

A retailer comparison covering margins, DuPont returns, liquidity, solvency and earnings quality.

Academic group projectHistorical analysis through 2025

Management question

Which retailer showed stronger profitability, capital efficiency and balance-sheet resilience through 2025?

12.03% NEXT net margin 2025
41.96% NEXT return on equity 2025
4.28% JD Sports net margin 2025
1.69x NEXT current ratio 2025
Finance view · NEXT led on profitability and return on equity

Conclusion

NEXT recorded a 12.03% net margin and 41.96% return on equity in 2025, compared with 4.28% and 14.53% for JD Sports. NEXT also had slightly higher 2025 asset turnover at 1.26x versus 1.15x.

NEXT's current and quick ratios were 1.69x and 0.98x, compared with 1.29x and 0.36x for JD Sports. JD Sports had the higher cash ratio at 0.51x versus 0.13x. This is a historical academic group project and no sole authorship is claimed.
