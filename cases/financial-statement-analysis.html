Financial statement analysis · NEXT plc and JD Sports Fashion plc

NEXT and JD Sports Financial Statement Analysis

Two UK retailers, three years of accounts. Margins, DuPont returns, liquidity, solvency, and by 2025 a fairly clear winner on the numbers.

Academic group projectFY2023 to FY2025 annual reports; sources accessed August 25, 2026

Operating question

Which retailer showed stronger profitability, capital efficiency and balance-sheet resilience through 2025?

12.03% NEXT net margin FY2025
4.28% JD Sports net margin FY2025
41.96% NEXT return on equity FY2025
33.22% NEXT debt to assets FY2025
NEXT led JD Sports on both margin and return on equity in 2025

What the comparison shows

NEXT produced a 12.03% net margin and 41.96% return on equity in 2025, compared with 4.28% and 14.53% for JD Sports. NEXT also recorded 1.26x asset turnover versus 1.15x for JD Sports.

NEXT's return on equity fell from 61.08% in 2023 as leverage declined, while debt to assets improved from 48.10% to 33.22%. The stronger 2025 profitability therefore came with a less leveraged balance sheet, although this remains a historical comparison rather than an investment recommendation.

Comparison limits

Limits to the comparison

  • NEXT and JD Sports do not report every measure on the same basis or on identical fiscal calendars.
  • Lease accounting, adjusted measures and business mix affect the comparison.
  • The analysis is historical and does not forecast either retailer.
