Deal view · EV / EBITDA is the primary control-value reference

Conclusion

Four profitable completed deals remain in the selected transaction-multiple set. Their median EV / EBITDA is 25.90x, which implies $43.54 per share for the illustrative target.

Nine completed deals inform the separate control-premium analysis, where the median is 33.0%. Terminated deals, loss-making observations and outliers are excluded from the selected multiple statistics.

What could change the answer

Risks and limitations

  • Target financials are illustrative.
  • Synchronized operating-metric periods and unaffected-price observation dates were not retained for every deal, so the 33.0% median premium is not independently reproducible or externally verified from the retained evidence.
  • Deal synergies and market conditions reduce comparability.
  • Precedents are backward-looking and should not be used alone.
