Rate shocks

The six standard shocks from the Basel rules for interest rate risk in the banking book, with the Basel Committee's euro sizes as recalibrated in July 2024: 225bp parallel, 350bp for short rates and 200bp for long rates. The shocks move the zero curve and every bond is repriced in full. The key-rate estimate is the first-order figure from the DV01s above. It leaves out convexity, and between key tenors it can only follow a shock's shape in straight lines.
