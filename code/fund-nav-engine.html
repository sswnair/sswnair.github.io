Fund NAV Engine

Calculates a fund's NAV every day for three share classes, then reconciles the books to the custodian and the brokers and ages every break. Fee accruals, a performance fee with a high-water mark, investor dealing and a daily NAV proof.

What the sample run shows

A made-up global equity fund starts the fourth quarter of 2025 with €180m in 14 stocks across four currencies. Over 63 valuation days it books 26 trades, 13 subscriptions and redemptions and 6 dividends. Net assets from the balance sheet equal the sum of the class NAVs, to the cent, on every one of them.

The performance fees crystallize on December 31: €460,299.24 on I-EUR and $416,469.65 on I-USD. Two redemptions crystallize their share of the fee when they leave, so the fee on those units doesn't drift back to the investors who stayed.

The custodian statements and broker confirmations carry 10 planted problems that produce 11 breaks, and the reconciliation finds all 11. Among them are a failed settlement, a custody charge the books never picked up, a dividend withheld at 30% instead of 15%, a mispriced confirm and a confirm for a trade the fund never made. Four are still open at year end, the oldest after 38 business days.

Work completed

What happens each day

The NAV

  • Trades on trade date, cash on settlement date
  • Holdings and cash valued in euros at the day's FX
  • Administration, depositary and audit accruals
  • The day's result split by prior class NAV
  • Management and performance fees per class
  • Forward-priced dealing at the day's NAV per share
  • The NAV proof against the balance sheet

The reconciliation

  • Settled positions against the custodian
  • Cash by currency, inside a tolerance
  • Trades against broker confirmations
  • A break register carried day to day
  • Ages in business days, bucketed
  • Stale price and large move checks

How the code is organized

data.py
Reads a fund's input files and downloads ECB reference rates
ledger.py
The books: positions, cash, amounts due and accrued fees
valuation.py
Holdings and cash in base currency
accruals.py
Daily fee accruals
classes.py
Splitting the result between classes, class NAV and NAV per share
perf_fee.py
Performance fee accrual and crystallization
dealing.py
Units issued and proceeds paid
reconcile.py
Positions and cash against the custodian, trades against the brokers
aging.py
The break register and business-day ages
engine.py
The daily run and the NAV proof

Limits

What it leaves out

  • No equalization: an investor who subscribes partway through the year bears the performance fee on gains made before they arrived.
  • Share classes are unhedged.
  • Income is cash dividends only. Splits, rights issues and other corporate actions are not booked.
  • Prices come from the pricing file as they are, with no fair value adjustments.
